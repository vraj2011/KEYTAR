Meet KEYTAR

A guitar you play with nothing but your keyboard. No strings, no tuning — just keys.

Pick a string

Play a note

Every letter key plays a different note on the string you picked. Try a few and listen the beats!

Follow the display

The screen always shows your string, your key, and the note it played — so you always know what you just heard. WITH "KEYTAR" YOU CAN BE A GREAT TYPER AND A MUSICIAN,TOO.
